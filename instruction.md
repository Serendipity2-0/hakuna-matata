### **Content Calendar Webpage with Enhanced Functionality and Visual Appeal**  

#### **Landing Page (Calendar View)** Use DB\Calendar.db to get the data
- A **large, interactive calendar** displaying the current month.  
- Each date box **highlights content posting days** with small icons representing content types (Reel, Post, Carousel, etc.).  
- **Hovering over a date** shows a **quick preview** of the scheduled content.  
- Clicking on a date **opens a detailed content card** in a sleek pop-up/modal.

#### **Content Card (On Date Selection)**  
When a user selects a date, a **beautifully designed pop-up card** appears with:  
1. **Content Type** – Displays whether the post is a **Reel, Carousel, or Standard Post** with relevant icons.  
2. **Platform(s)** – Lists the platforms where the content is scheduled (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, etc.).  
3. **View Content Button** – Clicking this opens the **actual content** (image/video/story) in a preview modal.  
4. **Edit Content Button** – Allows users to **modify the post**, change scheduling, or update platform selection.  
5. **Engagement Insights** – If the post has already been published, users can view basic engagement stats (likes, shares, comments).  

#### **Additional Functionalities & Features:**  
- **Color-coded categories** for different content types for easy recognition.  
- **Drag-and-drop rescheduling** of posts directly on the calendar.  
- **AI-based content suggestions** based on past engagement.  
- **Dark/Light Mode toggle** for a sleek interface.  
- **Collaboration Mode** – Team members can comment or suggest edits on scheduled posts.  
- **Quick Add Feature** – A floating ‘+’ button to **quickly add new content** without leaving the page.  
- **Search & Filter** – Users can search for content based on date, platform, or type.
