# Serendipity Accounts Guidelines

## Introduction
This document outlines the accounting procedures and guidelines for Serendipity. Following these guidelines ensures accurate financial reporting, compliance with regulations, and efficient financial operations.

## General Accounting Principles

### Accounting Method
- Serendipity uses the accrual basis of accounting
- Fiscal year runs from January 1 to December 31
- Monthly financial statements are prepared by the 15th of the following month

### Chart of Accounts
- Assets (1000-1999)
- Liabilities (2000-2999)
- Equity (3000-3999)
- Revenue (4000-4999)
- Expenses (5000-5999)
- Other Income/Expenses (6000-6999)

### Documentation Requirements
- All transactions must be supported by appropriate documentation
- Receipts must be retained for all expenses
- Invoices must be issued for all sales
- Documentation should be stored digitally with appropriate backup

## Revenue Recognition

### Service Revenue
- Recognize revenue when services are performed
- For ongoing services, recognize revenue over the service period
- Document the basis for revenue recognition in complex arrangements

### Product Sales
- Recognize revenue when products are delivered
- For digital products, recognize revenue upon customer access
- Record returns and allowances in the period they occur

## Expense Management

### Approval Process
- Expenses under $500 require department manager approval
- Expenses $500-$5,000 require director approval
- Expenses over $5,000 require CFO approval
- All capital expenditures require CFO approval regardless of amount

### Reimbursement Policy
- Submit expense reports within 30 days of incurring expenses
- Include all receipts and appropriate documentation
- Clearly indicate business purpose for all expenses
- Follow travel and entertainment policy guidelines

### Vendor Management
- All new vendors require W-9 or equivalent documentation
- Vendor setup requires approval from accounting department
- Competitive bids required for purchases over $10,000
- Vendor performance should be reviewed annually

## Financial Reporting

### Monthly Close Process
1. Record all revenue and expenses for the period
2. Reconcile all bank and credit card accounts
3. Review and adjust accruals as necessary
4. Prepare financial statements
5. Review financial statements with management

### Key Financial Reports
- Income Statement (P&L)
- Balance Sheet
- Cash Flow Statement
- Budget vs. Actual Report
- Accounts Receivable Aging
- Accounts Payable Aging

### Financial Analysis
- Gross margin by product/service line
- Operating expense ratio
- Current ratio and quick ratio
- Days sales outstanding
- Inventory turnover (if applicable)

## Tax Compliance

### Sales Tax
- Collect and remit sales tax in all jurisdictions where required
- File sales tax returns by required deadlines
- Maintain documentation of exempt sales

### Income Tax
- Maintain documentation to support all income tax positions
- Work with tax advisors to ensure compliance
- File all required tax returns by deadlines
- Make estimated tax payments as required

### Payroll Tax
- Ensure accurate withholding of employee taxes
- Remit payroll taxes by required deadlines
- File all required payroll tax returns
- Maintain documentation of contractor vs. employee classifications

## Internal Controls

### Segregation of Duties
- Different individuals should be responsible for:
  - Approving transactions
  - Recording transactions
  - Custody of assets
  - Reconciliation and review

### Cash Management
- Bank reconciliations performed monthly by someone independent of cash receipts/disbursements
- Dual approval required for wire transfers
- Check signing authority limited to appropriate personnel
- Petty cash reconciled weekly

### System Access
- Financial system access based on job responsibilities
- Regular review of user access rights
- Strong password requirements
- Immediate removal of access upon employee termination

## Audit Preparation
- Maintain organized documentation throughout the year
- Prepare schedules and analyses for key accounts
- Document significant accounting policies and estimates
- Respond promptly to auditor requests

## Conclusion
Adherence to these guidelines will ensure accurate financial reporting, regulatory compliance, and effective financial management for Serendipity.
