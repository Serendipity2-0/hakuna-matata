# Content Creation Guidelines for Dhoom Studios

## Introduction
This document provides guidelines for creating content for Dhoom Studios across various platforms. Following these guidelines ensures brand consistency and high-quality content.

## Brand Voice
- **Authentic**: Be genuine and transparent
- **Engaging**: Create content that sparks conversation
- **Informative**: Provide value through education and insights
- **Positive**: Maintain an uplifting and encouraging tone

## Content Types

### Social Media Posts
- Keep captions concise (150-200 characters for optimal engagement)
- Include 2-3 relevant hashtags
- Always include a call-to-action
- Respond to comments within 24 hours

### Blog Articles
- Length: 800-1500 words
- Include subheadings for easy scanning
- Add 1-2 relevant images
- Link to other relevant content
- End with a clear call-to-action

### Video Content
- Keep videos under 3 minutes for social media
- Include captions/subtitles for accessibility
- Start with a hook in the first 5-10 seconds
- End with a clear call-to-action
- Maintain consistent intro/outro branding

## Visual Guidelines

### Colors
- Primary: #FF5733 (Coral)
- Secondary: #33A8FF (Sky Blue)
- Accent: #FFD733 (Sunshine Yellow)
- Text: #333333 (Dark Gray)

### Typography
- Headings: Montserrat Bold
- Body text: Open Sans Regular
- Call-to-action: Montserrat Semi-Bold

### Images
- Use high-resolution images (minimum 1200px width)
- Maintain a consistent filter/editing style
- Ensure proper licensing for all images
- Include alt text for accessibility

## Content Calendar
- Plan content at least 2 weeks in advance
- Maintain a consistent posting schedule
- Align content with marketing campaigns and seasonal events
- Review performance metrics monthly and adjust strategy accordingly

## Approval Process
1. Content ideation and drafting
2. Internal review by team lead
3. Revisions based on feedback
4. Final approval by marketing director
5. Scheduling and publication

## Platform-Specific Guidelines

### Instagram
- Image ratio: 1:1 (square) or 4:5 (portrait)
- Stories: 9:16 ratio
- Use a mix of photos, carousels, and Reels
- Post 4-5 times per week

### YouTube
- Upload in 1080p or higher
- Optimize titles with keywords (60 characters max)
- Write detailed descriptions with timestamps
- Create custom thumbnails with consistent branding

### LinkedIn
- Focus on industry insights and company news
- Use a professional tone
- Include relevant statistics and data
- Post 2-3 times per week

## Measuring Success
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares)
- Reach and impressions
- Click-through rate
- Conversion rate
- Audience growth rate
