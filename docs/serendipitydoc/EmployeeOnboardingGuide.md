🔖 **Status Points for Employee Onboarding:**

### 1. 📞 FirstInterviewCompleted  
The candidate has successfully passed the initial interview, assessing their suitability for the role in terms of skills, experience, and cultural fit.

### 2. 📋 GoogleFormSent  
A Google Form is sent to the candidate to collect essential information such as contact details, address, and any necessary preliminary data. [Create a Google Form here](https://www.google.com/forms/about/).

### 3. 📝 EmployeeDetailsReviewed  
The collected data from the google form is reviewed by HR to verify the completeness and correctness of the information provided by the candidate, and also added in company employees detais for documentation.

### 4. 💾 GoogleFormDataSaved  
All the details gathered from the Google Form are securely stored in the company’s employee management system for future reference and onboarding procedures.

### 5. 🆔 PAN_Aadhar_Collected  
Official identity documents, including PAN card and Aadhar card, are collected from the employee. This ensures proper identification and compliance with legal requirements, scaned those documaintation and saved in company employees details.

### 6. 📄 ResumeSaved  
The candidate's resume is saved to the company’s HR system for reference during onboarding and to keep their career history on fileit in company employees details with pan and aadhar.

### 7. 🎉 WelcomeEmailSent  
A warm welcome email is sent to the candidate upon accepting the offer. It includes details such as the joining date, time, contact person, and any other preparatory information.

### 8. 📑 OfferLetterIssued (Signed by Employee and HR)  
The official offer letter is issued to the employee, and it is signed by both the employee and an HR representative to formalize the terms of employment and scanned that document and saved in offer Letter templete and filed it in company hard copy document.

### 9. 👥 TeamIntroductionCompleted  
The new employee is formally introduced to their team members, either virtually or in person, to help establish rapport and facilitate an easy transition into the team environment.

### 10. 📲 AddedToTelegramGroup  
The employee is added to the company's Telegram group to help them stay connected and updated on team activities, events, and important announcements.

### 12. ✉️ WelcomeEmailSent  
A follow-up welcome email is sent to provide more specific resources, such as links to onboarding documents, FAQs, and information.
