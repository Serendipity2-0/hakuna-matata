🔖 **Status Points for Employee Onboarding**:

1. 📞 **FirstInterviewCompleted**  - The candidate has successfully passed the initial interview, assessing their suitability for the role in terms of skills, experience, and cultural fit.

2. 📋 **GoogleFormSent**  - A Google Form is sent to the candidate to collect essential information such as contact details, address, and any necessary preliminary data. [Create a Google Form here](https://www.google.com/forms/about/).

3. 📝 **EmployeeDetailsReviewed**  - The collected data from the google form is reviewed by HR to verify the completeness and correctness of the information provided by the candidate, and also added in company employees detais for documentation .

4. 💾 **GoogleFormDataSaved**  - All the details gathered from the Google Form are securely stored in the company’s employee management system for future reference and onboarding procedures.

5. 🆔 **PAN_Aadhar_Collected**  - Official identity documents, including PAN card and Aadhar card, are collected from the employee. This ensures proper identification and compliance with legal requirements, scaned those documaintation and saved in company employees details.

6. 📄 **ResumeSaved**  - The candidate's resume is saved to the company’s HR system for reference during onboarding and to keep their career history on file it in company employees details with Pan and Aadhar.


7. 📑 **OfferLetterIssued** (Signed by Employee and HR)  - The official offer letter is issued to the employee, and it is signed by both the employee and an HR representative to formalize the terms of employment and scanned that document and saved in offer Letter templete and filed it in company hard copy document.

8. 🎉 **WelcomeEmailSent**  - A warm welcome email is sent to the candidate upon accepting the offer. It includes details such as the joining date, time, contact person, and any other preparatory information.

9. 👥 **TeamIntroductionCompleted**   - The new employee is formally introduced to different  Department like Serendipity,Dhoomstudio and Trademan team members, either virtually or in person, to help establish rapport and facilitate an easy transition into the team environment.

10. 📲 **AddedToTelegramGroup**   - The employee is added to the company's Telegram group to help them stay connected and updated on team activities, events, and important announcements like  Today I Learnt, In General Birthday Wishes,Leave,WFH,Delay. 

11. 📝 **EmployeeDetailsCollected** - The following Details to be collected from the Employees and documentation should be saved in Employees Details in Serendipity OneDrive-Accounting-Admin-Complete Employee Details Folder done by company HR. Then issue ID card for employees.

    • **Name**

12. ✉️ **WelcomeEmailSent** - A follow-up welcome email is sent to provide more specific resources, such as Serendipity or any other department Password to links to onboarding documents, FAQs, and information about upcoming events.

13. 🔑 **ToolsAccessGranted**  
    - The employee is granted access to the necessary tools, software, and platforms they will need for their role, including email, project management systems, and shared drives.

14. 🎥 **OrientationCompleted**  
    - The employee participates in an orientation session, either virtual or in-person, covering important company policies, culture, values, and expectations to help them get started. Employees daily active in Telegram group like Typing Test and Today I Learnt. This will make them more social and confident.

15. 🤝 **OnboardingBuddyAssigned**  
    - A mentor or buddy is assigned to the new employee. This experienced team member will help them navigate their first few weeks by answering questions and providing informal guidance.

16. 📚 **TrainingPlanProvided**  
    - A comprehensive training plan is provided to the employee, including online courses and internal resources. This helps them develop the skills needed to succeed in their new role.

17. 👥 **TeamIntroductionsDone**  
    - In addition to initial introductions, more in-depth meetings are organized with key colleagues, such as cross-functional team members and stakeholders, to help the new hire understand their role in the broader context.

18. 🎯 **InitialGoalsSet**  
    - Short-term goals are discussed and set with the new employee to provide them with clear objectives for the first few months. A manageable initial project is assigned to help them settle into their role.

19. 📊 **RegularCheckInsScheduled**  
    - Regular check-in meetings are scheduled during the first few weeks or months to assess progress, provide support, answer any questions, and offer constructive feedback. Guide them in company’s schedule of work like morning 10.30 am to 11.30 am typing test and learn about new things, update daily tasks in Telegram group in their own name, 11.30 am to 12.30 pm discussion about today’s work and 2.30 pm to 4.30 pm do their tasks. End of the day they have to update their tasks.

20. 🎉 **OnboardingCompleted**  
    - The onboarding process is formally completed, and the employee has successfully integrated into the team. This status signifies they are now fully operational in their role.

21. ⏳ **LongLeaveTaken**  
    - The employee takes an extended leave, and appropriate procedures are followed, including knowledge transfer and system access adjustment. Necessary documentation for leave approval is also handled and payroll settled.

22. 💼 **ReturningFromLeave**    
    - The employee returns from long leave. A re-onboarding process takes place, including refresher training on any new tools or processes and meetings to help them reintegrate with the team.

23. 🚪 **PermanentRelieving**  
    - If the employee leaves the company permanently, a formal resignation process is followed. It includes knowledge transfer, clearance formalities, an exit interview, and issuance of relieving and experience certificates.
