### Payment gateway comparision:

Detailed comparison of various payment gateways, including popular ones like PhonePe and Paytm, along with their services, transaction fees, and UPI availability.

| **Company Name** | **Services Provided** | **Transaction Fees** | **UPI Availability** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

### Notes:

1. **Transaction Fees**: Fees are indicative and may vary based on specific conditions, volume, or negotiated rates. Always check the latest pricing on the respective payment gateway's official website.
2. **UPI Availability**: All listed payment gateways support UPI, which is increasingly popular in India due to its ease of use and low transaction costs.
3. **Support**: Customer support quality can vary. Razorpay, Paytm, and PhonePe are known for having robust support systems. It’s recommended to look at user reviews and possibly trial the support yourself before committing.
4. **Additional Features**: Many of these gateways offer additional services such as analytics, fraud detection, multi-currency support, and more. Ensure to choose a gateway that aligns with your specific business needs.
