# Entity Registration on DLT Platform

DLT Registration,  recommend : Videocon, Airtel, and Reliance Jio,Although, they are imposing charges to register. Kindly apply on all three simultaneously, so that you can get the Header Approved on at least one of them, and then your SMS will not be impacted.

    Video: [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CPbV9WO14J4&t=119s](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CPbV9WO14J4&t=119s)

(Sample Authorization Letter-[http://m.9m.io/gr7wjod](http://m.9m.io/gr7wjod)) (In case the Director or Owner himself is doing this Registration, he needs to upload a document that shows he is the Director (MOA or GST).

    [no-reply@vmipl.in](mailto:no-reply@vmipl.in)
    
    (Also check SPAM and Email Firewall). If you still failed to find the Email, kindly your concern with
    
    [support](mailto:support@msg91.com)[@msg91.com](mailto:teamcrm@msg91.com)

[<Video:https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZOtG3JNphJc>](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZOtG3JNphJc)

**Entity Registration Process for Reliance Jio:**

**​**

**1. Signup Link:** [https://trueconnect.jio.com/#/](https://trueconnect.jio.com/#/)

**2. Form Filling:** Fill out the form and upload the required documents with the Authorization letter(Sample Authorization letter: [***Jio LOA](https://drive.google.com/uc?id=17BOYB0yv0XtUdcrIc8aU6NyWBkCLBDmz&export=download))***.

**3. Form Submission:** Submit the application and you will get the approval in a day or two.

**4. Headers Registration:** Once registration is approved, kindly log in and add your Headers (Sender ID) and it will be approved in a day or two.

- Login to the Jio DLT platform.
- On the top menu, click on Header SMS.
- Select Header SMS Registration there.
- Select Header Type- Others for Transactional SMS and Promotional for Promotional SMS.
- Select the Category of your Business.
- Type a 6 alphabet Sender Id (Header) that matches your Company Name.
- Submit the Sender Id (Header).
