# Adobe Production Workflow Plan

## 1. Adobe After Effects
**Primary tool for animation and visual effects**

### Key Elements & Effects
- **Opening Sequence**
  - Use **Particle World** effect for transitioning ancient scrolls
  - **Text Animation Presets** for revealing historical dates
  - **Page Turn** effect for Manusmriti and Arthashastra references

### Historical Scenes
- **Great Fire of London**
  - **CC Particle World** for fire effects
  - **Turbulent Displace** for smoke
  - Color correction using **Curves** and **Glow** effects
  - **3D Camera movement** for dramatic revealing shots

### Technical Specifications
- **Resolution:** 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Frame Rate:** 24fps
- **Composition Length:** 2 minutes

## 2. Adobe Premiere Pro
**Main editing software for final assembly**

### Audio Mixing
- Multiple tracks for:
  1. Voice-over narration
  2. Background music (adjustable -18db)
  3. Sound effects (-12db to -15db)
  4. Ambient sounds (-20db)

### Transition Effects
- **Cross Dissolves** (15-20 frames)
- **Dip to Black** for major time periods
- **Morph Cut** for seamless narrative flow

## 3. Adobe Audition
**Audio enhancement and sound design**

### Sound Processing
- **Vocal Enhancement:**
  - Parametric EQ
  - Light compression (2:1 ratio)
  - De-essing
- **Background Elements:**
  - Period-specific ambient sounds
  - Custom fire crackling effects
  - Earthquake rumble creation

## 4. Adobe Photoshop
**Asset preparation and image enhancement**

### Asset Creation
- Historical document textures
- Period-specific architectural elements
- Custom brushes for fire and smoke effects
- Background plates for compositing

## 5. Adobe Illustrator
Vector graphics and icons

### Graphics Package
- Timeline elements
- Insurance symbols
- Mathematical formulas and statistical graphics
- Location maps and architectural diagrams

## Workflow Order
1. Asset Preparation
   - Create/prepare all static images in Photoshop
   - Design vector elements in Illustrator
   - Build animation elements in After Effects

2. Audio Production
   - Record and clean narration in Audition
   - Create/mix sound effects
   - Prepare music bed

3. Animation and Effects
   - Create main animations in After Effects
   - Render in sections for easier management

4. Final Assembly
   - Import all elements to Premiere Pro
   - Final edit and timing adjustments
   - Color grading and final mix

## Export Settings
- Format: H.264
- Resolution: 1920x1080
- Frame Rate: 24fps
- Audio: AAC, 48kHz, Stereo
- Bitrate: 16-20 Mbps VBR, 2 pass
