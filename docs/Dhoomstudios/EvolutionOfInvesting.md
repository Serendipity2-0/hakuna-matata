# Share Market

## Story

### Script
First-person, two-minute narration capturing the essence of the trading journey from the 17th century to the modern day.

"Imagine a time, centuries ago, when investing meant more than tapping on a screen. Back then, merchants and sailors braved treacherous seas, often not knowing if they'd make it back. In bustling ports, eager investors would part with their hard-earned gold to fund perilous voyages for spices and goods. Profits weren’t guaranteed, nor was there any real assurance that their investment would even make it home. Every return was a victory—and a lesson in patience.

Then came the Industrial Revolution, a new era. With it, our ambitions grew. No longer were we funding single ships, but entire networks—railroads stretching across continents, factories transforming cities. We pooled resources, not just for quick profits, but to build massive projects that laid the foundation for the world we know today. Collective investment became a force of change, enabling massive undertakings like railways, bridges, and industries that would serve generations.

Now, look around today—where trades are made in seconds, and wealth is built with a few clicks on a smartphone. Digital screens flash with prices, and decisions are made in moments. But as we marvel at the ease of modern investing, we must remember the legacy behind it. Each investment is built on the struggles, risks, and collective hope of those who came before us.

Investing may be easier now, but the need for wisdom is timeless. Respect the journey of money, understand the stakes, and, above all, invest with knowledge and care."
