# Share Market

## Scene 1: The Modern Living Room

The setting is a modern, stylish living room. The two friends are comfortably seated on an L-shaped sofa, surrounded by sleek furniture, soft lighting, and a large window that lets in the golden afternoon sunlight. They are casually dressed, absorbed in their smartphones, which glow softly in their hands. A flat-screen TV is on in the background, playing a financial news channel, but they pay little attention to it.

**Friend 1:** *(Eyes glued to his phone, he leans forward, showing the screen to his friend with excitement)*  
Bro, do you think I should invest in this stock?  
*(The screen shows a stock trading app, the graph for a stock soaring upwards, with green numbers flashing positively.)*

**Friend 2:** *(Takes a quick glance at the phone, then nods confidently, leaning back into the sofa with an easy smile)*  
Yeah bro, invest around 80k on this. You’ll definitely make double in a few days. Trust me, it’s a no-brainer.  
*(He gives a casual wave of his hand, as if dismissing any concerns.)*

**Friend 1:** *(Eyes lighting up, he smiles broadly, clearly excited by the prospect of easy money)*  
Done! Thanks, bro. You’re the best.  
*(He eagerly taps the “Buy” button on his phone, and the camera zooms in to show his thumb pressing down, followed by the confirmation screen on the phone.)*

As the transaction completes, the room is suddenly filled with a strange gust of wind. The curtains flutter wildly, papers from a nearby desk fly up into the air, and the sound of wind howling fills the room.

The two friends exchange confused glances, their phones forgotten for a moment as they look around the room. The wind seems unnatural, and the air grows heavy, as if something extraordinary is about to happen.

Out of nowhere, an old man appears. He is dressed in weathered, old-fashioned clothes—faded tunic, rough trousers, and worn boots. His white beard flows down to his chest, and his hair is wild, as though he’s just stepped off a ship after a long journey. He looks around the room, his expression one of mild annoyance, as if he's been pulled from some faraway place unexpectedly.

## Scene 2: The Old Man's Arrival

**Old Man:** *(Wipes his brow theatrically, then sighs, exasperated)*  
Ah, things are so easy for these young men nowadays. *(He gestures broadly towards them)* Look at you, making profits with just a single click!  
*(His voice is rich, echoing slightly, as if coming from another time entirely.)*

The friends sit frozen, their eyes wide in shock, unsure whether they’re seeing a ghost, a time traveler, or something else entirely. One of them instinctively holds up his phone as if to protect himself, the other looks at the old man with a confused half-smile.

**Friend 2:** *(Still trying to process the scene, he raises an eyebrow)*  
Uh... who are you?

**Old Man:** *(Huffs, brushing off the question with a wave of his hand)*  
Who am I? I’m someone who’s seen the real struggles of investing! In my time, *(he leans in dramatically)* we had to cross vast seas, go days without food, and face storms just to earn a single share of profit!  
*(He shakes his head in disbelief at how easy things have become.)*

The two friends exchange glances again. One of them mouths the words “Is he serious?” to the other.

**Old Man:** *(Eyes narrowing at their skepticism, he straightens up, his voice deepening)*  
What! You don’t believe me? Come, let me show you the hardships we had to bear to enjoy the benefits of profit.

The camera begins to blur as the room starts to shimmer and fade. The walls of the modern living room melt away, replaced by the open seas, large wooden ships swaying in the waves. The colors shift to a more muted, sepia tone, as if stepping back in time to a place filled with both danger and opportunity.

## Scene 3: The Historical Journey

The scene changes to a bustling port in the 15th century. Sailors and merchants gather by the docks, overseeing the loading of crates onto large wooden ships. The sound of creaking timbers, seagulls calling, and waves crashing fills the air. The ships are setting out on long voyages, seeking spices and other valuable goods from distant lands.

**Narrator (Old Man’s Voice):** *(Voiceover)*  
Yes, this is how the share market began in our times. Men would stand in line, eagerly investing their hard-earned money in ships bound for perilous voyages. Profits weren’t calculated on screens or news bulletins. No, we had to wait months—sometimes years—to even know if our investments were still afloat!

The camera pans across the scene, showing anxious investors huddled together, handing over gold coins to captains and merchants, hoping their investments would return safely. Ships drift off into the horizon, with uncertainty hanging over them.

The screen then transitions from the cobblestone streets of the port to high-rise buildings slowly being constructed in the background. Time speeds up, showing the evolution of trade and commerce—markets grow busier, paper bonds and stocks are exchanged, industrial machinery begins to appear, symbolizing the dawn of the Industrial Revolution.

## Scene 4: The 19th Century

The old man is now standing at the open door of a moving train from the 19th century, looking out over vast tracks of newly laid railroads. Steam billows up from the engine as the train speeds along, carrying passengers and goods to new cities.

**Old Man:** *(With a look of pride)*  
Soon enough, people realized the power of investing together. Huge projects—factories, railways, bridges—were built with collective savings. We put everything we had into these ventures. Your modern conveniences *(he gestures to the surroundings)* are the result of those investments.

The camera zooms out to reveal sprawling factories, bridges spanning great distances, and bustling cities rising up. Workers and investors alike contribute to the growing economy, building the infrastructure that future generations would take for granted.

## Scene 5: The Present Day

The scene fades to the present day. The old man walks through a fast-paced modern trading floor, surrounded by digital screens displaying stock charts, numbers flashing in green and red. Traders on their phones make transactions in seconds. He pauses to observe how effortlessly people are making investments now.

**Old Man:** *(Turning back to the camera, with a soft sigh)*  
You see, we faced countless challenges just for a few more profits. What you achieve today with just one click, we had to cross oceans for.

The camera cuts back to the friends, sitting on their sofa, now watching the old man intently, their expressions more serious and thoughtful.

**Old Man:** *(With a knowing smile)*  
But remember, just because it’s easy doesn’t mean it’s risk-free. Always respect the value of money and the power of investing wisely.

The old man gives them a final look, full of wisdom, before slowly fading away, as mysteriously as he arrived.

## Scene 6: Reflection

The two friends sit in silence for a moment, processing what they’ve just experienced.

**Friend 1:** *(Turning to his friend, half-joking but clearly more thoughtful now)*  
Maybe I should rethink this investment?

**Friend 2:** *(Nods slowly, smiling but also a bit more serious)*  
Yeah... maybe we should do a bit more research first.

They both laugh, but it’s clear the conversation has taken on a deeper meaning.

The screen fades out as the words appear:

**"Investing has evolved, but wisdom is timeless. Invest responsibly."**

## HOW TO BRING THIS TO LIFE?

To effectively produce this expanded script using Adobe tools like Premiere Pro, After Effects, and Audition, here’s a step-by-step guide tailored to bring out the best in each scene:

### 1. Scene Setup and Editing (Premiere Pro)

**Scene 1 (Casual Conversation):**
- Start with clean cuts between the two friends as they converse. Use simple over-the-shoulder shots or medium close-ups to keep the casual, modern vibe. Ensure the room lighting is soft but warm, matching the laid-back tone.
- When Friend 1 shows the stock app on the phone, consider using a split-screen effect or an insert shot of the phone display for clarity. Use motion tracking (in Premiere Pro or After Effects) to focus the audience’s attention on the stock chart.
- Keep the background music low and subtle at this point—something modern and relaxed (like lo-fi or a soft ambient track). You can adjust the volume with keyframes in Premiere Pro to fade in and out at natural moments in the conversation.

**Old Man’s Entrance (Transition):**
- For the gust of wind, use slow-motion effects for added drama. You can film the curtains and papers fluttering and then slow down the footage in Premiere Pro. Use Adobe’s Warp Stabilizer to smooth any shaky shots and enhance the effect.
- Add a wind sound effect (import into Premiere Pro), adjusting the timing and intensity to match the visual cues.
- For the old man's sudden appearance, use After Effects for a subtle “fade-in” or “whirlwind” effect. You can create a masked dissolve transition with a layer of wind-like motion using particle simulations or dust effects in After Effects to make the old man’s entrance magical yet believable.

### 2. Visual Effects (After Effects)

**Old Man’s Introduction:**
- Apply a soft glow effect around the old man as he appears. This can be done in After Effects using the Glow effect (under the Effects panel), which can help him stand out in the scene as someone out of time.
- Add subtle environmental changes, such as a slight color shift or vignette (in Premiere Pro) when the old man speaks, creating a subconscious cue that something magical or important is happening.

**Historical Transition (15th Century Flashback):**
- When transitioning to the past, use a Gaussian blur effect in Premiere Pro as the screen starts to fade and transform into the historical scene. Combine this with time-lapse effects or a speed ramp to show the world moving rapidly from the present to the past.
- For the ships and sea voyages scene, use stock footage or render custom visuals in After Effects. You can apply color grading (sepia tones or desaturation) in Lumetri Color to give the historical scenes an old-world, vintage look.
- Camera tracking in After Effects will allow you to integrate the old man into historical scenes seamlessly. This will make him appear as if he’s walking alongside the crowd in the 15th century.

### 3. Motion Graphics (After Effects)

**Old Man’s Monologue:**
- During the narration, use motion graphics to visually represent key historical facts or milestones. For example:
  - Animate coins falling into a chest as he talks about early profits from voyages.
  - Create map animations showing trade routes and voyages across the seas. After Effects' motion tracking and shape layers are great for this.
  - Use text overlays to highlight important concepts like "The Industrial Revolution" or "Collective Investment." These can be done with text animations in After Effects, where the text appears or fades in time with the old man’s words.

**Time-lapse Construction of Buildings:**
- As the scene transitions from cobblestone streets to high-rise buildings, use 2D motion graphics or 3D layer animations in After Effects to simulate buildings rising and factories being constructed. You can use a scaling effect to show this gradual evolution quickly.

### 4. Color Grading (Premiere Pro)

**Tone Shifts Between Scenes:**
- **Scene 1 (Present Day):** Use a warm color palette to reflect the modern, laid-back environment. Keep the tones soft and vibrant.
- **Scene 2 (Historical Flashback):** Switch to cooler or sepia tones to suggest the past. You can do this in Lumetri Color by adjusting the temperature and adding slight grain for an old-film look. Use vignette effects to focus the audience's attention on specific actions, like sailors boarding ships or investors handing over coins.

**Scene Transitions (Past to Present):**
- Use gradual color shifts when transitioning back to the modern day. For example, as the old man moves from the past into the modern stock trading floor, brighten the colors and remove the sepia tones slowly, symbolizing the leap back into the present.

### 5. Sound Design (Audition)

**Old Man’s Appearance (Sound Effects):**
- In Adobe Audition, create a dramatic soundscape for the old man’s entrance. Layer different sound effects, such as wind gusts, magical chimes, or eerie whispers. Use reverb and echo to give the sound a mysterious quality.
- Adjust the old man’s voice slightly by adding a slight reverb in Audition, making it sound like his voice has a bit of “age” to it, adding depth to the character.

**Background Music & Transitions:**
- In Audition, layer the suggested background music tracks for different scenes. Use audio crossfades to blend the upbeat music in Scene 1 with the more dramatic orchestral music during the historical flashback.
- When transitioning between different eras, add appropriate soundscapes—for the past, layer in sounds of ships creaking, market chatter, and distant horns. For the Industrial Revolution, mix in mechanical noises like factory machinery and train whistles.

**Dialog Clarity:**
- Ensure all dialogue is clean and clear by using vocal enhancement effects in Audition. You can apply noise reduction to remove any background hum and equalize the voices for a crisper sound. Match audio levels so the dialogue stands out over the music and sound effects.

### 6. Title and End Message (After Effects)

**Final Message (Motion Graphics):**
- For the final message, “Investing has evolved, but wisdom is timeless. Invest responsibly,” create a sleek text animation in After Effects. Have the text appear letter by letter, with a soft glow or fade-in effect.
- Use keyframe animations for smooth transitions between different parts of the message. Keep the font bold yet elegant, reinforcing the theme of timeless wisdom.
- You can also add a subtle animated logo or branding at the end using motion paths and opacity keyframes in After Effects to give it a polished, professional touch.

### 7. Transitions and Effects (Premiere Pro & After Effects)

- Use dissolve transitions between scenes to avoid abrupt cuts, especially when moving between time periods.
- Use dynamic zooms in Premiere Pro for specific shots (like when the friends first see the old man or when the old man fades away).
- Motion blur effects can be applied during fast transitions (like the gust of wind or when the old man walks through history) for a smoother, more natural visual flow.
